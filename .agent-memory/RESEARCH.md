# Research

- **Task 4 · question.** When does direct transfer from confounded observations harm online learning, and when do causal bounds reduce exploration loss? The recorded study tests a one-step bandit. How to extend this evidence to sequential COOL or the reward-shaping path remains open. The bandit implementation in `causal_rl/algo/cool/cool.py` and the reward-shaping implementation have distinct scope.
- **Task 5 · hypothesis.** Graph-based restriction can lower exploration cost when it preserves an optimal legal intervention. Check candidate-set value gaps and both global and own-set regret. The six-variable legality constraint and collider settings test different failure modes of the comparison. Exact expected rewards are scoring oracles.
- **Task 6 · interpretation.** A paired backbone with and without the natural action measures the effect of changing the learner's information and representation. Use information-specific oracles, random-hint controls, and the calm CartPole control to separate information about the confounder from effects of learning with a different representation. Check that the state and intention are sufficient for the policy class when history matters. See [the run record](experiments/task6.md) for measured coverage.
- **Task 7 · planned.** The local lab protocol `Drafted Report/task4_7_main_experiment_protocol_2026-10-27.tex` (November 4 revision, outside this Git repository) describes four AntMaze/HumanoidMaze Medium/Large configurations, BC/GAIL/SQIL/IQ-Learn and their causal variants, and learner seeds 1000–1009. It specifies 2M executed interactions for SQIL and IQ-Learn, a round schedule for GAIL, and an offline BC reference. Older summaries differ on Race and on equal interaction budgets. Confirm the approved scope before using either design.
- **Task 7 · assumptions to verify.** Establish reliable experts and demonstrations, seeded resets, success on the final updated state, graph/input encoding, budget and evaluation accounting, and action-sequence replay across the proxy/sensor shift. Use runtime goal success and a shared evaluation bank to test within-pair robustness. A pure proxy shift requires equal physical trajectories and rewards under replay. Verify this before interpreting results. The local manuscript `overleaf_report/sections/tasks/task7.tex` has a results placeholder. Formal Task 7 results remain unverified.

For Tasks 4–7, the independent training run is the statistical unit. Preserve deliberate method pairing, label the score and metric units, and use oracle calculations to calibrate learnability separately from observed training outcomes.
